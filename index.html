Due dates

Know what's due before more money arrives

An envelope can say when its money has to be there: weekly, monthly, quarterly or yearly, on a day of the month, a weekday, or the last day. Afloat measures every one of them against your next payday, because when income is irregular the calendar month is the wrong unit.

A yearly bill gets saved up for a slice at a time. Fund $1,200 at $100 a month and you are hitting the target perfectly, and still $900 short in the month it actually leaves. Afloat counts both, and does the division for you.

The switch
